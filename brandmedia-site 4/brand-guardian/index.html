Brand Guardian · Ongoing Brand Management

Your brand doesn’t need another supplier.
It needs someone looking after it.

Brand Guardian gives established businesses ongoing senior creative oversight — without adding a full-time Brand Manager or Creative Director to the payroll.

Fractional brand managementOne experienced creative eye across the whole brand.
The role

Growth creates more marketing. It can also create more inconsistency.

As teams, suppliers and channels multiply, brand decisions become fragmented. Brand Guardian puts one experienced creative partner back at the centre — setting standards, guiding execution and keeping the whole picture aligned.

What Brandmedia oversees

From strategy to the details people actually see.

Brand Guardian can sit across day-to-day design, campaigns, websites, packaging, supplier output and AI-assisted content.

Typical scope

A senior creative resource that flexes with the business.

01

Brand direction

Advice on how the brand should evolve, what to prioritise and how new initiatives fit the broader identity.

02

Creative oversight

Review campaigns, layouts, websites and supplier output before inconsistent work reaches customers.

03

Design support

Ongoing creation and refinement of the collateral the business actually needs month to month.

04

System maintenance

Keep templates, brand assets, guidelines and AI instructions current as new channels and needs emerge.

05

Supplier coordination

Provide a clear design point of contact for printers, developers, marketers, photographers and other partners.

06

Quarterly health checks

Step back from production and review where brand consistency, messaging or customer experience is slipping.

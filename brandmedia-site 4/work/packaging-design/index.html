01 · ProLink

Branded box artworks for Farmers.

Challenge. Supply print-ready box artwork and imagery for social posts and marketing to supply the Farmers Trading Company retail chain.

Thinking. Apply Brandmedia's brand guidelines consistently across two homeware appliance ranges — Sheffield and Modello.

Solution. Generate InDesign-formatted artwork using PDFs for client proofing, then supply final high-resolution, print-ready Acrobat PDFs via Dropbox for the printer.

Outcome. Consistent branded packaging established a series of homeware appliances for the Farmers Trading Company retail chain.

02 · Remarkable Foods

PreBio: brand development & packaging.

Challenge. Develop a brand of probiotic drinks to be distributed through the New Zealand Foodstuffs supermarket chains.

Thinking. The project was broken into three stages — design a logo for the overall brand name, PreBio; design the bottle packaging and 6-pack shrink-pack outer; then generate print-ready artwork for the bottle, outer shrink-wrapping and a box shipper for bulk distribution.

Solution. Generate InDesign-formatted artwork using PDFs for proofing and final high-resolution, print-ready Acrobat PDFs downloaded from Dropbox for printing.

Outcome. After a rigorous application process from Foodstuffs NZ, Remarkable Foods secured a long-term supplier contract to deliver PreBio products throughout New Zealand — opening the door to further subsequent products already in development.

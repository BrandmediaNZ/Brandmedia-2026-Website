01 · Thinking

What makes a logo stand out.

This depends on the company, the intended message, the audience and the final design — but there are four essential components Brandmedia looks to incorporate into every mark it develops.

  • Audience-appropriate. The best logos aren't the flashiest, but the ones that resonate with their target audience — a logo represents not only the company, but the people it speaks to.
  • Easy to read. Particularly true of word-mark logos, but it applies to every design style — a mark should be understood at a glance.
  • Distinct. Industry trends are a good starting point, but the goal of a logo is to differentiate a brand from the competition. Distinct means memorable.
  • Scalable. A logo is placed across many media channels and sizes, so the best marks are versatile enough to fit any branding need.

About Brandmedia · Auckland NZ

Senior creative experience
without the agency overhead.

Brandmedia is an independent brand and digital design business led by Jeff Watkins, working directly with clients and bringing in trusted specialists where a project needs additional expertise.

Independent by designOne experienced creative partner. The right specialists when required.
20+years

Professional design experience across identity, print, packaging and digital.

Direct access to the person responsible for the work.

Brandmedia is deliberately structured differently from a traditional agency. Clients aren't passed between account managers, strategists and junior designers. The person discussing the brief is also deeply involved in the thinking and creative execution.

When a project needs specialist expertise, Brandmedia works with trusted independent partners — such as copywriters, developers, photographers, marketers and printers — while maintaining one clear creative direction.
